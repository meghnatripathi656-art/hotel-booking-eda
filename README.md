# Hotel Booking EDA

Exploratory Data Analysis of hotel bookings (2017-2019) using Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib and Seaborn.

## Business Questions
1. Which hotel type do customers book most often?
2. Does the length of stay affect the cancellation rate?
3. Does lead time affect the cancellation rate?

## Dataset
Hotel bookings from 2017 to 2019 (119,390 rows and 29 columns). After cleaning, 85,376 rows remain.

## Key Findings
- City Hotel accounts for 61.13% of bookings; Resort Hotel for 38.87%.
- October is the busiest month and March the quietest.
- At City Hotel, cancellations rise with longer stays (22.36% for 1 night to 45.98% for 8 nights).
- At City Hotel, cancellations rise with lead time (10.63% for 0-7 days to 52.82% for 365+ days).
- Resort Hotel is more stable in both cases.
